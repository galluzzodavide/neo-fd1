## Documents

- **`Tinygfm.pdf`**: draft of the thesis methodology (6 pages). It defines the scope and the research question, and describes the datasets (SSL4EO-S12, CloudSEN12, Sen1Floods11, THRawS), the conversion from pixel masks and boxes to patch labels, group-level splits and leakage checks, and the evaluation metrics. It also compares the teacher models (Copernicus-FM and Prithvi-EO-2.0) and sets out the distillation objective: cosine vs MSE loss, multi-task fine-tuning, and the size budget with INT8 quantisation. Chapters 4–10 are placeholders still to be written.
- **`code-testing.ipynb`**: a small end-to-end test of the NEO-FD1 pipeline on real data. It loads Sen1Floods11 and CloudSEN12 chips, cuts them into 16×16 patches with patch labels, applies group splits and training-only normalisation, and extracts teacher features with Copernicus-FM. It then distils a tiny CNN student with cosine and MSE losses (Phase 1), fine-tunes two task heads (Phase 2), and compares FP32, INT8 and INT4 quantisation in size and PR-AUC. It is a functional test, not thesis results.
